    Drop files here or click to browse JPG · PNG · WebP · GIF · AVIF · PDF · DOCX · XLSX · PPTX · ODT · TXT · MD · CSV · HTML

    Images

    EXIF, XMP, IPTC, comments, C2PA / Content Credentials and embedded prompts (Stable Diffusion, ComfyUI). Lossless, colour profile kept.

    Documents

    Author, company, generating app, template and custom properties in Office & OpenDocument files, plus their embedded images.

    PDF

    Info dictionary (Title, Author, Creator, Producer…), XMP metadata for the document and each page, and attached C2PA manifests.

    Text

    Zero-width and special spaces, bidi controls, hidden Unicode tags, steganographic selectors and chat leftovers like oaicite.

    What it can’t remove: invisible watermarks embedded in the pixels themselves or in the statistical pattern of the words (e.g. SynthID). Those aren’t metadata — they’re part of the content.
